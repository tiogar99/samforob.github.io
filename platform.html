My Platform

Building an Oak Bay for Everyone

My goal: build Oak Bay into an equitable, prosperous, safe, and sustainable community while and enhancing the District's natural beauty and architectural heritage.

My

Platform Priorities

Click each section to learn more.

01 Healthcare +

Other municipalities in the region are leaders in making sure residents can get care—Colwood has built a clinic and is hiring family doctors, and Victoria has committed to doing the same. It's time for Oak Bay to take action as well. In addition to advocating for a seniors campus of care at the Oak Bay Lodge site, I will work to bring a new clinic to Oak Bay, and make sure every resident has a family doctor. If the model proves successful, I will support hiring specialists for the outpatient care Oak Bay residents desperately need and can't get.

In addition to a community clinic and family doctors for all, I will work towards delivering a seniors campus of care on the old Oak Bay Lodge site. As an aging community with declining access to healthcare services, Oak Bay and the Capital Region as a whole needs these investments to avert suffering and deliver a good standard of life.

02 Affordability +

Oak Bay residents deserve an affordable places to live, affordable ways to get around, and easy access to affordable services. I will prioritize bringing affordable housing to Oak Bay, building out a complete walk, bike, and roll network, as well as bringing better transit to the District.

Because of the poor state of Oak Bay's infrastructure, the District is in a difficult position regarding building affordability. Putting new housing on a block with a chronically broken sewer main or intermittent water supply is foolhardy. I commit to a clear and transparent process for legalizing a variety of housing across the district, in lockstep with the necessary replacements to sewer and water mains.

03 Infrastructure +

Due to flagrant financial mismanagement, Oak Bay now has the highest property taxes in the Province. High taxes may be worth it, if they come with good services. Instead, Oak Bay is stuck with declining services and an infrastructure maintenance backlog of more than $300 million dollars. I commit to fixing Oak Bay's infrastructure quickly and efficiently. At the same time, I will work to spread out sudden tax increases and build a sustainable tax base.

04 Climate and Community Safety +

Mirroring Saanich, I will push for a municipal interest-free loan program to help property owners replace outdated furnaces with heat pumps, insulate their homes, and build cooling-off spaces in rental properties.
