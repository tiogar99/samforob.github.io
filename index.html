The Platform Get Involved

Democracy is about choice; leadership is about results. I'm running for Mayor because I think Oak Bay needs both. I believe Oak Bay deserves better access to healthcare, excellent transit, affordable housing, and safe cycling and walking in Oak Bay.

My focus as a community organizer and professional has always been about bringing people together to tackle complex problems—tackling bike theft, building housing affordability, and getting better transit for our city, to name a few of the problems I've helped organize around. I'm overjoyed to have been part of projects like creating our region's first professional bike valet program and tackling low vacancty rates by legalizing thousands of new homes across the Capital Region.

Looking Ahead

Key Platform Priorities

Four core focuses for our town's next four years.

01

Smart Growth & Housing Choice

Encourage responsible development that preserves municipal character while adding housing options for young families, seniors, and local workers.

02

Core Infrastructure First

Prioritize reliable water systems, road paving, sidewalk maintenance, and stormwater management over non-essential legacy projects.

03

Public Safety & Community Care

Work closely with local emergency services and regional health partners to address root causes of public safety issues and maintain vibrant public spaces.

04

Fiscal Discipline & Transparency

Keep property tax hikes reasonable by streamlining municipal operations and holding public town halls before major budget decisions.
