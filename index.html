Proven Experience

A Track Record of Getting Things Done

Over the past several years, I’ve worked alongside residents, small business owners, and local community groups to advocate for pragmatic solutions that improve daily life in our town.

My focus has always been on bringing people together, asking hard questions about municipal budgets, and ensuring local government remains accountable to the people it serves.

  • Infrastructure & Roads: Championed long-overdue road resurfacing and active transportation safety upgrades.
  • Fiscal Accountability: Advocated for transparent budget audits and responsible municipal property tax management.
  • Community Spaces: Led local fundraising efforts to preserve regional green spaces and upgrade neighborhood park amenities.

Looking Ahead

Key Platform Priorities

Four core focuses for our town's next four years.

01

Smart Growth & Housing Choice

Encourage responsible development that preserves municipal character while adding housing options for young families, seniors, and local workers.

02

Core Infrastructure First

Prioritize reliable water systems, road paving, sidewalk maintenance, and stormwater management over non-essential legacy projects.

03

Public Safety & Community Care

Work closely with local emergency services and regional health partners to address root causes of public safety issues and maintain vibrant public spaces.

04

Fiscal Discipline & Transparency

Keep property tax hikes reasonable by streamlining municipal operations and holding public town halls before major budget decisions.
