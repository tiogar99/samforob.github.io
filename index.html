About Sam

Why I'm Running

Democracy is about choice; leadership is about results. I'm running for Mayor because I think Oak Bay needs both. I believe Oak Bay deserves better access to healthcare, excellent transit, affordable housing, and safe cycling and walking.

My focus as a community organizer and professional has always been about bringing people together to tackle complex problems—tackling bike theft, building housing affordability, and getting better transit for our city, to name a few of the problems I've helped organize around. I'm overjoyed to have been part of projects like creating our region's first professional bike valet program and tackling low vacancy rates by legalizing thousands of new homes across the Capital Region.

Looking Ahead

Key Platform Priorities

Four core focuses for Oak Bay's next four years.

01

Climate

Climate change is here. Just a few weeks ago, I stood in the normally extremely wet forests on the North of the Island, breathing smoke, and feeling ash settle on my face. The heat dome killed far too many in our community. We need bold action to protect Oak Bay residents from smoke and heat, as well as reduce emissions. Mirroring Saanich, I will push for a municipal interest-free loan program to help property owners replace outdated furnaces with heat pumps, insulate their homes, and build cooling-off spaces in rental properties.

02

Healthcare

I don't have a family doctor. My mom has waited for more than a year for a hip surgery that keeps her from doing the things she loves, and the date keeps getting pushed back. My friend with chronic medical conditions are left to self-manage medications because they can't get in to see a doctor more than once or twice a year. These are the kinds of problems I want to see local electeds working to fix. Other municipalities in the region are leaders in making sure residents can get care—Colwood has built a clinic and is hiring family doctors, and Victoria has committed to doing the same. It's time for Oak Bay to take action as well. In addition to advocating for a seniors campus of care at the Oak Bay Lodge site, I will work to bring a new clinic to Oak Bay, and make sure every resident has a family doctor. If the model proves successful, I will support hiring specialists for the outpatient care Oak Bay residents desperately need and can't get.

03

Affordability

Even my friends who earn well above average can't afford to raise kids without significant help from family. Many people my age are choosing not to have kids at all. I want to be able to afford to raise a family in this community. I am fully invested in lowering the cost of living in Oak Bay. Municipalities have an enormous role in this. As Mayor I will push for affordable housing, as well as more housing options across the District, so people can raise families and age in place without needing to move outside the District. I will work to lower transportation costs by pushing for better transit, safe cycling and walking, and accessible small shops and grocers in neighbourhoods, so not every trip to the store requires a car. I will also work to spread out increases to property tax by increasing the tax base.

04

Infrastructure

Due to flagrant financial mismanagement, Oak Bay now has the highest property taxes in the Province. High taxes may be worth it, if they come with good services. Instead, Oak Bay is stuck with declining services and an infrastructure maintenance backlog of more than $300 million dollars. I commit to fixing Oak Bay's infrastructure quickly and efficiently. At the same time, I will work to spread out sudden tax increases and build a sustainable tax base.
